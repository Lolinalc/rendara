## How property managers usually charge

You will mainly find two models in the market:

- **Percentage of the rent.** The manager charges a percentage of what the tenant pays each month. This is the most common model.
- **Flat monthly fee.** A fixed amount regardless of the rent.

Some managers also charge separately for certain services. That's why, beyond the percentage, it's worth asking **exactly what the fee includes**.

## The detail that matters most: agreed rent vs. collected rent

When the fee is a percentage, there is a difference that is rarely explained:

- **On the agreed rent:** the manager charges based on what the lease says, even if the tenant hasn't paid.
- **On the collected rent:** the manager only earns when the money actually arrives.

At Rendara we charge **only on rent actually collected**. That way our incentive is the same as yours: for the rent to arrive in full and on time.

## What to ask before hiring any property manager

Whether you choose Rendara or not, these questions will help you compare:

1. Is the fee calculated on the **agreed** rent or the **collected** rent?
2. Which services are included and which are charged separately?
3. Who approves repair expenses, and up to what amount?
4. How often will I receive a report, and what information does it include?
5. Who is my direct contact, and how quickly do they respond?
6. What happens if I want to end the service?

## Is paying for management worth it?

Do the math: how many hours a month do you spend today collecting rent, answering tenant messages, finding vendors and checking on the property? Now add what a problem that gets solved too late costs you, like an unattended leak or a payment that is several months behind.

If that time and that risk are worth more than the fee, delegating pays off. And if you live outside Mérida or own several properties, the math usually tips even further.
